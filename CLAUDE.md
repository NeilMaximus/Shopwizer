# Repository notes for Claude

This repository holds two separate things:

- The Shopwizer AI commerce demo (repository root: `App.tsx`, `components/`, `index.html`). Its own Pearl BlueStar styling applies there.
- The Growth Lab website in `growth-lab/`, the editable source of the live growthlab.co.za site (released 14 October 2026 through NeilMaximus/Growth-Lab-2026; see `growth-lab/CLAUDE.md`). Any Growth Lab website work must follow `growth-lab/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` and `growth-lab/CLAUDE.md`, which set durable rules: Geist Sans only with no monospace anywhere, a white hero and page, the four ways section kept black, a quiet left to right client logo strip of genuine logos at the foot of the hero, white research, workshop and getting going sections after the stages, then the leading solutions logo row, the header and closing "Let’s Talk" actions both opening the contact view, a contact form that sends for real through `growth-lab/site/api/contact.mjs` or says it cannot, light grey only for supporting panels, the verified logo and rune, the search and sharing tags, robots file and sitemap, and the brand tokens in `growth-lab/tokens/`. Figures, sources and contact details live in `growth-lab/SOURCES.md`.

Keep the two apart: Growth Lab rules do not apply to Shopwizer, and Shopwizer styling does not apply to Growth Lab.
