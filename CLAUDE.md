# Repository notes for Claude

This repository holds two separate things:

- The Shopwizer AI commerce demo (repository root: `App.tsx`, `components/`, `index.html`). Its own Pearl BlueStar styling applies there.
- The Growth Lab website in `growth-lab/`. Any Growth Lab website work must follow `growth-lab/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` and `growth-lab/CLAUDE.md`, which set durable rules: Geist Sans only with no monospace anywhere, a white hero and page, the four stage section kept black, white evidence, workshop and audit sections after it with one "Book an audit" action, light grey only for supporting panels, the verified logo and rune, and the brand tokens in `growth-lab/tokens/`. Figures and their sources live in `growth-lab/SOURCES.md`.

Keep the two apart: Growth Lab rules do not apply to Shopwizer, and Shopwizer styling does not apply to Growth Lab.
