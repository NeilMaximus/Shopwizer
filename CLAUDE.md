# Repository notes for Claude

This repository holds two separate things:

- The Shopwizer AI commerce demo (repository root: `App.tsx`, `components/`, `index.html`). Its own Pearl BlueStar styling applies there.
- The Growth Lab website in `growth-lab/`. Any Growth Lab website work must follow `growth-lab/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` and `growth-lab/CLAUDE.md`, which set durable rules: Geist Sans only with no monospace anywhere, a white base with opaque grey panels, the verified logo and rune, and the brand tokens in `growth-lab/tokens/`.

Keep the two apart: Growth Lab rules do not apply to Shopwizer, and Shopwizer styling does not apply to Growth Lab.
