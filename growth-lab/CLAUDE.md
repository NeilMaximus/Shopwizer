## Hard rules

- **Typeface: Geist Sans only**, self hosted from `site/assets/fonts/geist/`. Never use Martian Mono, Geist Mono, Instrument Sans or any monospace or terminal styling in website UI, including navigation, labels, buttons, form fields and the footer. The four ways to work carry no 01 to 04 numbers.
- **Colours come from `tokens/tokens.css`.** Brand purple `#7761FF`, brand coral `#FF4E4E`, amber `#FEBC2E` (very sparing). `#FF5F57` is not brand coral. Coral text only at large sizes on white, with one recorded exception, the regular weight first sentence of the research introduction (`DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` section 11); white text on coral only as the 19 px bold action label.
- **Section grounds are fixed.** Header, hero, rune band and footer are white. The four ways section ("Four ways to work with Growth Lab": Fix, Grow, Automate, Decide) stays **black `#111111`**: white heading, four columns, each read top to bottom (service, promise, what you get, what we look at, the two labels with small purple box and eye icons), purple icon tiles and no arrows, coral names, purple soft offer names, white promises, light grey detail, all in Geist. Keep its copy short. Light grey (`--gl-panel`) is only for supporting sections, boxes and panels, such as the contact form panel, and never replaces the black section. No translucent, frosted or blurred surfaces.
- **Logo:** only `site/assets/brand/growthlab-logo.svg`, the verified SVG from growthlab.co.za (viewBox `0 0 297 70`). Never retype, redraw or stretch it. In the header 36 px high (30 px on phones) inside the 76 px header (68 px on phones) on every page; in the footer 30 px (26 px on phones).
- **Email logo:** `site/assets/brand/growthlab-email-logo-v1.png`, rendered unchanged from that SVG (`scripts/render_email_logo.cjs`), lives at a stable public URL used in email signatures. Never overwrite v1; a changed logo is a new v2 file.
- **Rune:** paths 1 and 2 of that logo, geometry unchanged (`growthlab-rune.svg`). Faint grey, oversized, cropped by the hairline above the homepage footer, slow breathing motion only (a 12 s breath, already moving as the band enters view).
- **Keep the value cube and its slow loop** (build, rest, fold back, grow again; version 2.12), with no pause button (Neil, 16 October 2026; a recorded WCAG 2.2.2 exception, `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` section 14). Honour `prefers-reduced-motion`. The only other motion is the rune's breath, the approved logo strip and the research carousel when the visitor moves it.
- **Header and footer:** the header holds the logo and one dark "Let’s Talk" pill, with no menu. The homepage footer holds the logo, copyright, the Privacy and Website terms links and the LinkedIn icon (Neil, 14 October 2026), nothing else; the contact view's footer has no logo.
- **White sections after the black section** (research, workshops, getting going) follow `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` section 11 and `SOURCES.md`, with Neil's approved copy word for word (version 2.12, 15 October 2026). Research: "What 1,634 real martech stacks can tell you about yours." and the first sentence of the introduction in brand coral, the rest of the introduction, "What it tells you" and three bullets in dark text; the research carousel (Neil, 16 October 2026): MartechTribe's Apex Martech Matrix retail example rebuilt in Growth Lab colours from `config/apex-retail-example.json` (where you sit, with retailers South Africans know highlighted and the axes in MartechTribe's terms, Martech presence and Martech performance; what each position means, in four cards of one size with icons; the illustrative Apex Score, 63 to 87, credited as based on MartechTribe's score, and what to fix first as a clear table, with plain line icons and never vendor logos; never a score for a named company; purple controls), then the MartechMap whole, with only its small credit as caption and one restrained coral glow. The carousel never moves on its own, and `scripts/render_share.cjs` renders the same examples as WhatsApp images in `share/whatsapp/`; then MartechTribe's four figures as one quiet row split by hairlines (no tiles, boxes or counters) and "Research used by 200+ global brands, including" followed by four small genuine logos in ink (Standard Bank, Vodafone, Philips, Unilever, from `site/assets/brands/`), smaller than the client logos at the top. The map is not a chart of the benchmark: keep the figures and the 49 categories out of its caption. Attribute the research and both workshops to MartechTribe. The only approved superlative is "the world’s largest independent benchmark of real martech stacks", to be confirmed with MartechTribe. The retail example lives only in the carousel, credited to MartechTribe and the CMO Council; no pricing or testimonials. Workshops stay fully open (no accordion), Technology Strategy first: name, title, description, pull quotes, then "Ideal for" and "You’ll leave with". After the people, "We work across the leading solutions" with platform logos in ink (`config/platform-logos.json`, `site/assets/platforms/`): genuine artwork only, smaller than the client logos, centred, with room before the rune; never call Growth Lab a partner of any of them unless the vendor confirms it.
- **Client logo strip** at the foot of the hero (`DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` section 10): genuine logos only, from the sources in `SOURCES.md`, never redrawn, typeset or substituted; a slow left to right loop that holds still on hover and off screen, with no pause button (Neil, 16 October 2026; the same recorded exception), and a still wrapping version for reduced motion. The build shows only files present in `site/assets/clients/` and names the missing ones.
- **Actions:** the dark "Let’s Talk" pill (header) and the coral "Let’s Talk" (getting going) both open `contact.html`; there is no other action. No email address, phone number or copy action on the homepage. Never claim a confirmed booking, a price or free offer, an exclusive partnership, or that Frans attends every audit. Neil and Frans keep identical 96 px portraits and separate roles.
- **Contact view and form** follow `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` section 12: "Where could your business perform better?" as a real headline (three balanced lines beside the form), hello@growthlab.co.za and Neil's phone +27 84 504 0505 (`tel:+27845040505`, from `config/contact.json`) side by side, five labelled fields, Growth Lab's own Google reCAPTCHA v2 checkbox and a coral "Let’s Talk" submit. Never reuse another site's reCAPTCHA key, phone number or copy.
- **Sending is real or it is off.** `site/api/contact.mjs` sends only to the fixed hello@growthlab.co.za, verifies reCAPTCHA on the server, validates every field again, keeps secrets in environment variables, logs outcome codes only and fails closed. Never add a fake send, a fake checkbox or a mailto fallback that pretends to submit. The page says a message was sent only when the server confirms it, and the preview builds say plainly that nothing is sent.
- **Copy:** UK English, concise, Neil's approved words. No em dashes.
- **Legal honesty:** never invent entity details, registration numbers, addresses or compliance claims. The privacy notice and website terms were approved and published on 14 October 2026 (Future Island (Pty) Ltd trading as Growth Lab, effective 14 October 2026): their text is `legal/PRIVACY_NOTICE.md` and `legal/WEBSITE_TERMS.md`, and `site/privacy.html` and `site/terms.html` are the published pages exactly as live. Change the Markdown and the page together, and only with Neil's approval.
- **Search and sharing** (`DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` section 18, version 2.12.1): every public page keeps its title, description, canonical address and link preview tags; the homepage's structured data uses only details from the approved privacy notice, with no email or phone; `site/robots.txt` and `site/sitemap.xml` list the public pages; render `growthlab-og.png` again with `scripts/render_og_image.cjs` when the headline changes. Keep the page free of layout shift and logo files at most 72 px high. The research story fits one window at 1920 x 1080, 1536 x 864 and 1440 x 900 (`DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` section 11).
- **Do not deploy, set up accounts or keys, change the live growthlab.co.za site or its DNS or MX records, or buy anything** unless Neil asks. Releases go through the Growth-Lab-2026 repository as above.

## Workflow

1. Edit tokens in `tokens/tokens.css`, legal text in `legal/*.md`, contact details in `config/contact.json`, pages in `site/`.
2. `python3 scripts/build.py preview` syncs tokens, checks the approved legal pages, renders the phone line, writes the artifact preview to `dist/preview/` (email and phone shown as text, because the Claude viewer can go blank after such a link; the contact form in preview mode; publish `contact.html`, the legal pages and `assets/media/` with it) and writes `dist/browser/`, complete pages with images built in that open straight from a folder in any browser.
3. `node tests/verify.cjs` (Playwright) unit tests the contact function with Google and Resend mocked, and checks fonts, mono free text, logo and rune geometry, tokens, header height, section grounds and contrast on black, the exact copy, layout, images and ratios, keyboard details, the actions, the contact form in every state (not connected, validation, success, check failed, provider failed, preview), click targets, focus, reduced motion, both animations and overflow at 1440, 1280, 834, 390 and 320 px, the search and sharing tags, robots and sitemap, and the research fit on the most used desktop screens, and saves screenshots to `dist/screenshots/`. All checks must pass.
4. When a figure, source or contact detail changes, update `SOURCES.md`, the page and its as at date together.
5. `python3 scripts/build.py package` writes the production build (`dist/production/`, with the approved legal pages), `dist/growth-lab-site.zip` and `dist/growth-lab-2026/` with its ZIP, the layout for the Growth-Lab-2026 repository (see `DEPLOY.md`).
