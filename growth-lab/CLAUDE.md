# Growth Lab website: instructions for Claude

This folder holds the Growth Lab website (Make Your Martech Pay, its contact view and the pages that follow). Read `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` before changing anything here. Its rules are durable and supersede earlier Growth Lab website styling.

## Hard rules

- **Typeface: Geist Sans only**, self hosted from `site/assets/fonts/geist/`. Never use Martian Mono, Geist Mono, Instrument Sans or any monospace or terminal styling in website UI, including navigation, labels, buttons, form fields and the footer. The four stages carry no 01 to 04 numbers.
- **Colours come from `tokens/tokens.css`.** Brand purple `#7761FF`, brand coral `#FF4E4E`, amber `#FEBC2E` (very sparing). `#FF5F57` is not brand coral. Coral text only at large sizes on white; white text on coral only as the 19 px bold action label.
- **Section grounds are fixed.** Header, hero, rune band and footer are white. The four stage section (Diagnose, Prioritise, Unlock, Optimise) stays **black `#111111`**: purple icon tiles and arrows, coral headings, white taglines, light grey body text, all in Geist. Light grey (`--gl-panel`) is only for supporting sections, boxes and panels, such as the contact form panel, and never replaces the black section. No translucent, frosted or blurred surfaces.
- **Logo:** only `site/assets/brand/growthlab-logo.svg`, the verified SVG from growthlab.co.za (viewBox `0 0 297 70`). Never retype, redraw or stretch it. 30 px high (26 px on phones) in a 76 px header (68 px on phones) on every page.
- **Rune:** paths 1 and 2 of that logo, geometry unchanged (`growthlab-rune.svg`). Faint grey, oversized, cropped by the hairline above the homepage footer, slow breathing motion only.
- **Keep the value cube and its build animation.** Honour `prefers-reduced-motion`. The only other motion is the approved logo strip.
- **Header and footer:** the header holds the logo and one "Book an audit" pill, with no menu. The footer holds the logo, copyright and the legal links, nothing else.
- **White sections after the stages** (research, workshops, getting going) follow `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` section 11 and `SOURCES.md`, with Neil's approved copy word for word. The Deloitte or Accenture line stays a question: never add prices, timelines or claims about those firms. No Sources dropdown or separate proof line. The MartechMap is shown whole with its credits; its caption repeats the research sentence with "1,634 benchmarked martech stacks" in bold, then the small credit. The map is not a chart of the benchmark. Attribute the research and both workshops to MartechTribe, write "since 2017", never attach the 49 categories to the map. No retail example, stat tiles, superlatives, pricing or testimonials.
- **Client logo strip** at the foot of the hero (`DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` section 10): genuine logos only, from the sources in `SOURCES.md`, never redrawn, typeset or substituted; a slow left to right loop with a pause control, hover and focus pause, and a still wrapping version for reduced motion. The build shows only files present in `site/assets/clients/` and names the missing ones.
- **Actions:** "Book an audit" (header) and the coral "Let’s Talk" (getting going) both open `contact.html`. No email address, phone number or copy action on the homepage. Never claim a confirmed booking, a price or free offer, an exclusive partnership, or that Frans attends every audit. Neil and Frans keep identical 96 px portraits and separate roles.
- **Contact view and form** follow `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` section 12: "Where could your business perform better?" as a real headline (three balanced lines beside the form), hello@growthlab.co.za and Neil's phone +27 84 504 0505 (`tel:+27845040505`, from `config/contact.json`) side by side, five labelled fields, Growth Lab's own Google reCAPTCHA v2 checkbox and a coral "Let’s Talk" submit. Never reuse another site's reCAPTCHA key, phone number or copy.
- **Sending is real or it is off.** `site/api/contact.mjs` sends only to the fixed hello@growthlab.co.za, verifies reCAPTCHA on the server, validates every field again, keeps secrets in environment variables, logs outcome codes only and fails closed. Never add a fake send, a fake checkbox or a mailto fallback that pretends to submit. The page says a message was sent only when the server confirms it, and the preview builds say plainly that nothing is sent.
- **Copy:** UK English, concise, Neil's approved words. No em dashes.
- **Legal honesty:** never invent entity details, registration numbers, addresses or compliance claims. Privacy and terms stay marked as drafts until `PRELAUNCH.md` is cleared.
- **Do not deploy, set up accounts or keys, change the live growthlab.co.za site or its DNS, or buy anything** unless Neil asks.

## Workflow

1. Edit tokens in `tokens/tokens.css`, legal text in `legal/*.md`, contact details in `config/contact.json`, pages in `site/`.
2. `python3 scripts/build.py preview` syncs tokens, renders the draft legal pages and the phone line, writes the artifact preview to `dist/preview/` (email and phone shown as text, because the Claude viewer can go blank after such a link; the contact form in preview mode; publish `contact.html`, the legal pages and `assets/media/` with it) and writes `dist/browser/`, complete pages with images built in that open straight from a folder in any browser.
3. `node tests/verify.cjs` (Playwright) unit tests the contact function with Google and Resend mocked, and checks fonts, mono free text, logo and rune geometry, tokens, header height, section grounds and contrast on black, the exact copy, layout, images and ratios, keyboard details, the actions, the contact form in every state (not connected, validation, success, check failed, provider failed, preview), click targets, focus, reduced motion, both animations and overflow at 1440, 1280, 834, 390 and 320 px, and saves screenshots to `dist/screenshots/`. All checks must pass.
4. When a figure, source or contact detail changes, update `SOURCES.md`, the page and its as at date together.
5. `python3 scripts/build.py package` writes `dist/growth-lab-site.zip` for Vercel, with `api/` and `.env.example`.
