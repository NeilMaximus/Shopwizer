# Growth Lab website: instructions for Claude

This folder holds the Growth Lab website (Make Your Martech Pay, its contact view and the pages that follow). Read `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` before changing anything here. Its rules are durable and supersede earlier Growth Lab website styling.

## Live site

Since 14 October 2026 this site is live on growthlab.co.za (release 2.11, source commit 5e3f6bc). Vercel project `growth-lab-2026` deploys it from `main` of NeilMaximus/Growth-Lab-2026, which holds only the build output: `public/`, `api/contact.mjs` and `vercel.json`, copied from `dist/growth-lab-2026/`. This folder stays the editable source. Every commit to that repository's `main` goes straight to production; rollback is Vercel Instant Rollback. The release is archived in the GrowthLab shared drive under Brand Assets / Website / 2026-10-14 Production Release. `handover/` holds the CLAUDE.md for that repository and a memory note for the Claude app.

## Hard rules

- **Typeface: Geist Sans only**, self hosted from `site/assets/fonts/geist/`. Never use Martian Mono, Geist Mono, Instrument Sans or any monospace or terminal styling in website UI, including navigation, labels, buttons, form fields and the footer. The four ways to work carry no 01 to 04 numbers.
- **Colours come from `tokens/tokens.css`.** Brand purple `#7761FF`, brand coral `#FF4E4E`, amber `#FEBC2E` (very sparing). `#FF5F57` is not brand coral. Coral text only at large sizes on white; white text on coral only as the 19 px bold action label.
- **Section grounds are fixed.** Header, hero, rune band and footer are white. The four ways section ("Four ways to work with Growth Lab": Fix, Grow, Automate, Decide) stays **black `#111111`**: white heading, purple icon tiles and arrows, coral names, white promises, purple soft offer names, light grey detail, all in Geist. Light grey (`--gl-panel`) is only for supporting sections, boxes and panels, such as the contact form panel, and never replaces the black section. No translucent, frosted or blurred surfaces.
- **Logo:** only `site/assets/brand/growthlab-logo.svg`, the verified SVG from growthlab.co.za (viewBox `0 0 297 70`). Never retype, redraw or stretch it. In the header 36 px high (30 px on phones) inside the 76 px header (68 px on phones) on every page; in the footer 30 px (26 px on phones).
- **Email logo:** `site/assets/brand/growthlab-email-logo-v1.png`, rendered unchanged from that SVG (`scripts/render_email_logo.cjs`), lives at a stable public URL used in email signatures. Never overwrite v1; a changed logo is a new v2 file.
- **Rune:** paths 1 and 2 of that logo, geometry unchanged (`growthlab-rune.svg`). Faint grey, oversized, cropped by the hairline above the homepage footer, slow breathing motion only (a 12 s breath, already moving as the band enters view).
- **Keep the value cube and its build animation.** Honour `prefers-reduced-motion`. The only other motion is the approved logo strip.
- **Header and footer:** the header holds the logo and one dark "Let’s Talk" pill, with no menu. The homepage footer holds the logo, copyright and the legal links, nothing else (the production build leaves the legal links out until approved policies exist); the contact view's footer has no logo.
- **White sections after the black section** (research, workshops, getting going) follow `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` section 11 and `SOURCES.md`, with Neil's approved copy word for word (version 2.12, 15 October 2026). Research: "What 1,634 real martech stacks can tell you about yours.", the introduction, "What it tells you" and three bullets, all dark text; the MartechMap whole, with only its small credit as caption and one restrained coral glow; then MartechTribe's four figures as one quiet row split by hairlines (no tiles, boxes or counters) and the 200+ brands line. The map is not a chart of the benchmark: keep the figures and the 49 categories out of its caption. Attribute the research and both workshops to MartechTribe. The only approved superlative is "the world’s largest independent benchmark of real martech stacks", to be confirmed with MartechTribe. No retail example, pricing or testimonials. Workshops stay fully open (no accordion), Technology Strategy first: name, title, description, pull quotes, then "Ideal for" and "You’ll leave with".
- **Client logo strip** at the foot of the hero (`DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` section 10): genuine logos only, from the sources in `SOURCES.md`, never redrawn, typeset or substituted; a slow left to right loop with a pause control, hover and focus pause, and a still wrapping version for reduced motion. The build shows only files present in `site/assets/clients/` and names the missing ones.
- **Actions:** the dark "Let’s Talk" pill (header) and the coral "Let’s Talk" (getting going) both open `contact.html`; there is no other action. No email address, phone number or copy action on the homepage. Never claim a confirmed booking, a price or free offer, an exclusive partnership, or that Frans attends every audit. Neil and Frans keep identical 96 px portraits and separate roles.
- **Contact view and form** follow `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` section 12: "Where could your business perform better?" as a real headline (three balanced lines beside the form), hello@growthlab.co.za and Neil's phone +27 84 504 0505 (`tel:+27845040505`, from `config/contact.json`) side by side, five labelled fields, Growth Lab's own Google reCAPTCHA v2 checkbox and a coral "Let’s Talk" submit. Never reuse another site's reCAPTCHA key, phone number or copy.
- **Sending is real or it is off.** `site/api/contact.mjs` sends only to the fixed hello@growthlab.co.za, verifies reCAPTCHA on the server, validates every field again, keeps secrets in environment variables, logs outcome codes only and fails closed. Never add a fake send, a fake checkbox or a mailto fallback that pretends to submit. The page says a message was sent only when the server confirms it, and the preview builds say plainly that nothing is sent.
- **Copy:** UK English, concise, Neil's approved words. No em dashes.
- **Legal honesty:** never invent entity details, registration numbers, addresses or compliance claims. Privacy and terms stay marked as drafts until `PRELAUNCH.md` is cleared.
- **Do not deploy, set up accounts or keys, change the live growthlab.co.za site or its DNS or MX records, or buy anything** unless Neil asks. Releases go through the Growth-Lab-2026 repository as above.

## Workflow

1. Edit tokens in `tokens/tokens.css`, legal text in `legal/*.md`, contact details in `config/contact.json`, pages in `site/`.
2. `python3 scripts/build.py preview` syncs tokens, renders the draft legal pages and the phone line, writes the artifact preview to `dist/preview/` (email and phone shown as text, because the Claude viewer can go blank after such a link; the contact form in preview mode; publish `contact.html`, the legal pages and `assets/media/` with it) and writes `dist/browser/`, complete pages with images built in that open straight from a folder in any browser.
3. `node tests/verify.cjs` (Playwright) unit tests the contact function with Google and Resend mocked, and checks fonts, mono free text, logo and rune geometry, tokens, header height, section grounds and contrast on black, the exact copy, layout, images and ratios, keyboard details, the actions, the contact form in every state (not connected, validation, success, check failed, provider failed, preview), click targets, focus, reduced motion, both animations and overflow at 1440, 1280, 834, 390 and 320 px, and saves screenshots to `dist/screenshots/`. All checks must pass.
4. When a figure, source or contact detail changes, update `SOURCES.md`, the page and its as at date together.
5. `python3 scripts/build.py package` writes the production build (`dist/production/`, without the draft legal pages), `dist/growth-lab-site.zip` and `dist/growth-lab-2026/` with its ZIP, the layout for the Growth-Lab-2026 repository (see `DEPLOY.md`).
