# Growth Lab website design system

Version 2.12, 15 October 2026. Owner: Neil Botha.

These rules govern every page of the Growth Lab website, starting with Make Your Martech Pay and its contact view. They are durable: follow them by default in every new page, edit and review. Neil requested and approved the changes recorded here. Change a rule only when he asks for it, and record the change in the change log at the end.

Values live in `tokens/tokens.css` (with a JSON copy in `tokens/tokens.json`, regenerated by the build). Never hard code a colour, font or radius in a page. Add a token instead.

## 1. Rules that supersede earlier ones

Version 2 replaces the earlier website styling, which came from the Life Insurer Lens page and the presentation system. Version 2.1 then corrects 2.0: the four stage section stays black. For the website, these older rules no longer apply:

| Earlier rule | Now |
| --- | --- |
| Instrument Sans for reading | Geist Sans (Google Fonts family name `Geist`) for everything |
| Martian Mono for labels, eyebrows, numbering and navigation | No monospace face anywhere. Labels and navigation are Geist Sans, and the stages carry no numbers |
| Uppercase, widely tracked small labels | Sentence case, restrained tracking (0 to minus 0.005em) |
| Black header bar with a typed "Growth Lab" lockup | White header with the real Growth Lab vector logo |
| Off white canvas `#F2F2F0` | White page and hero `#FFFFFF`, with opaque very light grey `#F5F5F5` for supporting sections, boxes and panels |
| Black bands throughout | One deliberate black section, the four stages, on `#111111`. Version 2.0's "make the whole page white" is withdrawn |
| Dotted margin marks | Retired on the website |
| Coral `#FF5F57` | Brand coral is `#FF4E4E`. `#FF5F57` is only the window control red of the live site's OS chrome and is not a brand colour |

The presentation system for decks keeps its own rules. This document covers the website.

## 2. Principles

1. Concise copy. Every word earns its place. Do not pad, add slogans or invent claims.
2. A white hero and page with generous space and a clear hierarchy, inspired by the restraint of x.ai/bot, plus one deliberate black section for the four ways to work. The contact view takes its directness from Isoflow's contact page: a clear heading, a few large labelled fields and one button. Draw inspiration only. Never copy either site's layout, typeface, assets, keys or contact details.
3. One bold device per section: the value cube in the hero, the purple nodes that head each of the four ways to work, the MartechMap beside the research, the rune above the footer. Everything around them stays quiet, including the client logo strip at the foot of the hero.
4. Solid surfaces only. Panels are opaque. No translucency, frosted glass, blur, glows or heavy shadows, with one approved exception: the restrained coral glow behind the MartechMap (section 11).
5. Motion with a purpose, slow and controlled. Never spinning, bouncing or decorative noise.
6. One kind of action. Every action on the homepage leads to the contact view.

## 3. Typography

**Typeface: Geist Sans only.** Self hosted from Vercel's official `geist` npm package, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`site/assets/fonts/geist/OFL.txt`).

- File: one variable woff2, `Geist-Variable-Latin.woff2` (about 24 KB). It is limited to weights 400 to 700 and the Latin range. Rebuild it with `scripts/build_font.py` if more glyphs or weights are ever needed.
- Loading: `font-display: swap`, preloaded in the page head. A size matched local fallback (`Geist Fallback`, built on Arial with `size-adjust: 105.46%`) keeps the layout steady while the font loads.
- Stack: `var(--gl-font)`, which is Geist, then Geist Fallback, then the system sans stack. Form fields and buttons inherit it.
- **No monospace anywhere in website UI.** That includes headlines, body, navigation, small labels, buttons, form fields, the footer and any numbering. Do not use Martian Mono, Geist Mono or any terminal styling.

- Never turn the four ways section white or light grey, and never restore Martian Mono, uppercase mono labels or the 01 to 04 numbering in it.
- Light grey is for supporting sections, boxes and panels only. It never replaces the black stage section.
- Do not add other black or coloured bands without Neil's approval.
- Panels are solid `--gl-panel` with a 28 px radius (`--gl-radius-panel`). Never translucent.
- Content width 1200 px with a fluid gutter of 20 to 48 px. The hero, the black four ways section and the legal pages use `--gl-section` (72 to 140 px); the white sections after the stages use 56 to 96 px above and below, with a hairline between them.
- Apart from the black stage section, separate sections with space and hairlines (`--gl-line`).
- Radii: panels 28 px, stage nodes and portraits 14 px, form fields and notices 12 px, framed images 10 px, buttons fully rounded.
- Every layout works from 320 px wide with no horizontal scroll.
