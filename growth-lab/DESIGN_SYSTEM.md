# Growth Lab website design system

Version 2.11, 14 October 2026. Owner: Neil Botha.

These rules govern every page of the Growth Lab website, starting with Make Your Martech Pay and its contact view. They are durable: follow them by default in every new page, edit and review. Neil requested and approved the changes recorded here. Change a rule only when he asks for it, and record the change in the change log at the end.

Values live in `tokens/tokens.css` (with a JSON copy in `tokens/tokens.json`, regenerated by the build). Never hard code a colour, font or radius in a page. Add a token instead.

## 1. Rules that supersede earlier ones

Version 2 replaces the earlier website styling, which came from the Life Insurer Lens page and the presentation system. Version 2.1 then corrects 2.0: the four stage section stays black. For the website, these older rules no longer apply:

| Earlier rule | Now |
| --- | --- |
| Instrument Sans for reading | Geist Sans (Google Fonts family name `Geist`) for everything |
| Martian Mono for labels, eyebrows, numbering and navigation | No monospace face anywhere. Labels and navigation are Geist Sans, and the stages carry no numbers |
| Uppercase, widely tracked small labels | Sentence case, restrained tracking (0 to minus 0.005em) |
| Black header bar with a typed "Growth Lab" lockup | White header with the real Growth Lab vector logo |
| Off white canvas `#F2F2F0` | White page and hero `#FFFFFF`, with opaque very light grey `#F5F5F5` for supporting sections, boxes and panels |
| Black bands throughout | One deliberate black section, the four stages, on `#111111`. Version 2.0's "make the whole page white" is withdrawn |
| Dotted margin marks | Retired on the website |
| Coral `#FF5F57` | Brand coral is `#FF4E4E`. `#FF5F57` is only the window control red of the live site's OS chrome and is not a brand colour |

The presentation system for decks keeps its own rules. This document covers the website.

## 2. Principles

1. Concise copy. Every word earns its place. Do not pad, add slogans or invent claims.
2. A white hero and page with generous space and a clear hierarchy, inspired by the restraint of x.ai/bot, plus one deliberate black section for the four stages. The contact view takes its directness from Isoflow's contact page: a clear heading, a few large labelled fields and one button. Draw inspiration only. Never copy either site's layout, typeface, assets, keys or contact details.
3. One bold device per section: the value cube in the hero, the line of stage nodes, the MartechMap beside the research, the rune above the footer. Everything around them stays quiet, including the client logo strip at the foot of the hero.
4. Solid surfaces only. Panels are opaque. No translucency, frosted glass, blur, glows or heavy shadows, with one approved exception: the restrained coral glow behind the MartechMap (section 11).
5. Motion with a purpose, slow and controlled. Never spinning, bouncing or decorative noise.
6. One kind of action. Every action on the homepage leads to the contact view.

## 3. Typography

**Typeface: Geist Sans only.** Self hosted from Vercel's official `geist` npm package, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`site/assets/fonts/geist/OFL.txt`).

- File: one variable woff2, `Geist-Variable-Latin.woff2` (about 24 KB). It is limited to weights 400 to 700 and the Latin range. Rebuild it with `scripts/build_font.py` if more glyphs or weights are ever needed.
- Loading: `font-display: swap`, preloaded in the page head. A size matched local fallback (`Geist Fallback`, built on Arial with `size-adjust: 105.46%`) keeps the layout steady while the font loads.
- Stack: `var(--gl-font)`, which is Geist, then Geist Fallback, then the system sans stack. Form fields and buttons inherit it.
- **No monospace anywhere in website UI.** That includes headlines, body, navigation, small labels, buttons, form fields, the footer and any numbering. Do not use Martian Mono, Geist Mono or any terminal styling.

## 5. Surfaces, layout and sizing

**Section grounds (fixed rule, Neil, 14 October 2026):**

| Section | Ground |
| --- | --- |
| Header | White |
| Hero ("Make your martech investment pay.") | White. The value cube sits on a light grey panel inside it, and the client logo strip runs along its foot, on white |
| Four stages (Diagnose, Prioritise, Unlock, Optimise) | **Black `#111111`, full width.** Purple icon tiles and connecting arrows, coral headings, white semibold taglines, light grey body text, no numbers. All Geist Sans |
| Evidence, workshops and getting going (after the stages) | White, separated by hairlines inside the content width. No cards, tiles or grey panels. See section 11 |
| Rune band | White, with the faint grey rune cropped by the hairline |
| Footer | White |
| Contact view | White, with the form on one light grey panel. See section 12 |
| Future supporting sections | White, or light grey `--gl-panel` sections, boxes and panels |

- Never turn the four stage section white or light grey, and never restore Martian Mono, uppercase mono labels or the 01 to 04 numbering in it.
- Light grey is for supporting sections, boxes and panels only. It never replaces the black stage section.
- Do not add other black or coloured bands without Neil's approval.
- Panels are solid `--gl-panel` with a 28 px radius (`--gl-radius-panel`). Never translucent.
- Content width 1200 px with a fluid gutter of 20 to 48 px. The hero, the black stages and the legal pages use `--gl-section` (72 to 140 px); the white sections after the stages use 56 to 96 px above and below, with a hairline between them.
- Apart from the black stage section, separate sections with space and hairlines (`--gl-line`).
- Radii: panels 28 px, stage nodes and portraits 14 px, form fields and notices 12 px, framed images 10 px, buttons fully rounded.
- Every layout works from 320 px wide with no horizontal scroll.

## 10. Components

- **Header:** white, 76 px (68 px on phones). The real logo on the left and one dark "Let’s Talk" pill on the right that opens the contact view, `contact.html`. No menu or section links. The contact view's header shows the logo only, at the same height, linking home.
- **Buttons:**
  - Dark pill (`--gl-ink`, white 15 px 500 text, 44 px high) for "Let’s Talk" in the header (it read "Book an audit" until version 2.6).
  - Coral pill (`--gl-coral`, white 19 px 700 text, 52 px high, `--gl-coral-hover` on hover) for "Let’s Talk", in the getting going section and as the contact form's submit, where it runs full width under 640 px. White on coral is 3.25:1, so the label must stay 19 px bold or larger. Never put smaller or lighter text on coral.
  - Every link, button and summary is at least 24 px each way, and both pills at least 44 px high.
- **Four stage section:** black `#111111`, full width, `--gl-section` padding top and bottom. Coral heading, white tagline (Geist 600, two sentence taglines break between sentences), body in `--gl-on-black`. No numbers.
- **Client logo strip:** at the foot of the white hero, before the black stages, as quiet credibility rather than an extra panel. Rules:
  - **Logos:** the twelve brands in `config/client-logos.json` (Sanlam, KFC, Old Mutual, Philips, Santam, Sportsmans Warehouse, Momentum, Famous Brands, Telesure, Multiply, Budget Insurance, First for Women). Each is a genuine logo from the source recorded in `SOURCES.md`, as a clean black variant on transparent, in `site/assets/clients/`. Never redraw, retype, stretch, crop or substitute a sub brand. A brand without a genuine file is left out and named as missing by the build and in the page source. White "knockout" files (a white box with the logo cut out, invisible on white) are converted with `scripts/knockout_to_black.py`, which removes the box without touching the logo's outlines.
  - **Size:** one stable row height (30 px logos on desktop, 24 px on phones, in a row 1.6 times that), with a per logo `scale` in the manifest to even out optical weight. Gaps of 48 to 80 px (40 px on phones). No cards, labels or claims.
  - **Space:** 64 to 112 px above the strip; the hero's bottom padding drops to 56 to 96 px when it is there.
  - **Motion:** one row drifting left to right, slow and constant: 20 CSS px a second on desktop, 18 under 640 px, linear, endlessly. The duration is calculated from the measured width of one set, and the track holds enough identical copies that travelling exactly one set returns to an identical picture, so there is no acceleration or visible reset. Soft 7 percent edge fades. It rests while off screen.
  - **Control:** a small "Pause" button (13 px, ink 3, 32 px high) below the strip on the right, which becomes "Play"; its accessible name is "Pause the client logos" or "Play the client logos". The strip also holds still on hover and while the control has focus.
  - **Still version:** with reduced motion or without the script, there are no copies and no control, and the logos stand still, centred and wrapping.
  - **Screen readers:** one list labelled "Clients", one image per brand with the brand name as alt text. Copies are `aria-hidden`, `inert` and have empty alt text.
- **Footer:** white, restrained. On the homepage: the logo, then the legal line (copyright, Privacy, Website terms and the "Drafts, pending approval" note), under the rune band. On the contact view: the legal line only, with no logo (the header already carries it, Neil, version 2.7), starting with a hairline and aligned to the content's left edge. No section links, email pill or copy button.
- **Offline copies:** the private Claude preview and the browser files cannot send email. Their contact view runs in preview mode (section 12). The Claude preview also shows the email address and phone number as plain text, because the artifact viewer can go blank after an email or phone link, and points links home to `index.html`. The browser files keep working email and phone links. `scripts/build.py` makes these changes and fails if they stop matching.
- **Draft legal pages:** generated from `legal/*.md`, marked "Draft for review. Not approved and not yet in force", `noindex`, with CONFIRM placeholders highlighted.

**Honest states.** The page says a message was sent only when the server confirms it.

**Server rules (`api/contact.mjs`).**

- The recipient is fixed: hello@growthlab.co.za. It can never come from the request.
- Every field is validated again on the server, with the same rules and messages as the page.
- The reCAPTCHA token is verified with Google before anything is sent, and its hostname is checked when `CONTACT_ALLOWED_HOSTNAMES` is set.
- One plain text email is sent through Resend, with the visitor's address as reply to.
- It fails closed on missing settings, a failed or unreachable check, and a provider that refuses or cannot be reached. Nothing is sent and the page is told why.
- No secrets reach the page: only the public site key. Logs carry outcome codes only, never the visitor's details or token. A hidden honeypot field and a 32 KB body limit turn away simple bots.

## 13. Motion

- **Cube build:** on load, after 0.5 s, the cube grows from the core along one axis at a time (0.72 s each, cubic ease in and out), lighting each outcome label and arrow as its axis grows. It then rests.
- **Rune breathing (version 2.6):** the two chevrons drift apart diagonally by 1.6 units, about 5 percent of the rune's width (39 px at full size, 16 px on a phone), and back, 6 s each way, so a full breath is 12 s (`--gl-rune-cycle`), on a gentle sine ease (`--gl-ease-rune`). Each chevron starts 40 percent into its breath, so the drift is already visible when the band appears rather than easing out of a standstill. It runs as soon as any part of the band is within 120 px of the viewport, keeps running while partly visible, and rests only once the band has fully left. Its rest position is the true logo geometry, and 6 percent headroom above the rune keeps the larger drift uncropped. No spin, bounce, flash or faster speeds.
- **Client logo strip:** a slow, constant drift from left to right, approved by Neil for version 2.5. Details in section 10.
- **Reduced motion:** with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, the cube shows its finished state at once, the rune stays still and the client logos stand still and wrap. Smooth scrolling is off.
- No other animation without Neil's approval. Never spin, bounce or loop attention grabbing effects.

## 14. Accessibility

- Forms: visible labels, autocomplete, required fields marked for assistive technology, readable errors linked to their fields, status messages in live regions, and focus moved to the first error or to the confirmation.
