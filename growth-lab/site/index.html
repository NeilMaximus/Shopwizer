What 1,634 real martech stacks can tell you about yours.

Through MartechTribe, Growth Lab brings the world’s largest independent benchmark of real martech stacks to South African businesses.

See how your technology compares with relevant peers, where you could get more from existing platforms and which investments deserve priority.

Each business is placed on two dimensions: martech presence, the capabilities of its technology, and martech performance, the organisation’s ability to use them.
