## What we collect

When you use our contact form, we collect your full name, company, work email address and message. Your telephone number is optional. Please avoid including sensitive personal information or confidential client data in your enquiry.

These details come directly from you. The required fields help us understand and respond to your enquiry. If you prefer not to use the form, you can email or telephone us instead.

Our hosting and security providers may also process technical information, including IP addresses, browser and device information, and requests to the website, to deliver and protect the service.

## Why we use your information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, discuss your requirements, manage any resulting working relationship and protect the website against spam and abuse. Depending on the purpose, processing is necessary to take steps towards an engagement at your request, perform an agreement, pursue legitimate business and security interests, or comply with the law. Where consent is required, we will ask for it.

Submitting an enquiry does not sign you up to a newsletter. We do not sell your personal information or use the form to make automated decisions about you.

## Service providers and sharing

The website is hosted by **Vercel**. Contact enquiries are sent through **Resend** to our **Google Workspace** mailbox. The website does not maintain a separate enquiry database; copies may remain in email systems and provider logs.

We use **Google reCAPTCHA** to help distinguish genuine enquiries from automated abuse. It may process device, browser and interaction information and use cookies or similar technologies. Its use is subject to [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms).

We share information with people and providers who need it to handle your enquiry or provide these services, and where disclosure is required by law. The website currently has no advertising trackers or marketing analytics. Fonts and brand images are served from our own website.
